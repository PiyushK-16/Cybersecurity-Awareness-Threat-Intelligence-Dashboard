Five words that are not interchangeable

ObservationSomething was seen. Not yet validated.
IndicatorA validated artifact (IP, domain, URL, hash...). A match is not proof of compromise.
AlertAn indicator that crossed a rule threshold and needs analyst triage.
ThreatCorroborated, higher-confidence activity with related indicators.
IncidentAn analyst-confirmed security incident. Rare by design.

Risk is not confidence

Risk says how concerning something may be. Confidence says how much we trust the intelligence. Risk 90 with confidence 25 means "potentially serious, but the evidence is weak".
